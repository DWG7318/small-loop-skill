# SLK 4.2.0 Optional Overwatcher Design

**Date:** 2026-09-22
**Status:** Owner-approved design; ready for TDD implementation

## Purpose

SLK 4.2.0 reduces cross-project rule drift, makes abnormal stops observable and recoverable, and removes misleading BI activity without replacing the existing method. Supervisor, Checker, Worker, CELL, D0, D1, D2, rework, SLK TOKEN, and their direct communication remain authoritative.

The release adds at most one optional Overwatcher Agent Session per Run. It observes operation, retries an unchanged message to its original endpoint when mechanically safe, and escalates genuine decisions. It is not a Router, scheduler, engineering approver, BI editor, or required dependency.

## Fixed topology

```text
Supervisor <-> Checker <-> Worker
     ^            ^           ^
     +------ optional Overwatcher observation/recovery ------+

work facts + transport evidence + observations -> read-only projections -> LE BI
```

- Supervisor owns planning, role selection, exceptions, material replanning, and D2.
- Checker owns CELL readiness, dispatch, isolated D1, and rework requests.
- Worker owns the current CELL implementation, D0, and candidate delivery.
- Overwatcher owns operational observation, exact-message recovery, and escalation evidence only.
- LE BI owns no fact and exposes no mutation surface.

Routine CELL traffic remains direct between Checker and Worker. Checker returns Run-boundary matters to Supervisor through the existing route. Overwatcher never becomes the mandatory relay and its absence cannot stop a valid Run.

## Binding and lifecycle

Supervisor may explicitly bind zero or one Overwatcher to a Run. The binding records a dedicated role identity, exact Agent Session endpoint, model binding, reason, and time. The same Overwatcher Session cannot be bound to another Run and cannot be silently replaced, inferred from titles, or selected by recent activity.

The Overwatcher may be provided by Codex, LCaS, or another Agent with the required read, exact-send, evidence, and archive capabilities. It remains non-authoritative even when its Session belongs to a capable Agent. On terminal Run closure it records closure, removes any one fallback wake schedule it owns, and archives its Session. A missing or failed Overwatcher leaves the original three-role Loop usable.

## Two ledgers, one authority chain

The state core separates records without duplicating engineering truth:

1. **Work facts** are authored by Supervisor, Checker, and Worker: plans, assignments, D0, D1, D2, candidates, rework, blockers, and terminal decisions.
2. **Operational observations** are authored by Overwatcher: delivery/start evidence, activity not proven, exact-message retry, recovery escalation, record conflict, projection refresh request, and closure.

Observations reference existing `event_id`, `message_id`, Run scope, evidence, and plan revision. Normal cycles stay sparse: Overwatcher records boundaries by reference and writes detail only for an exception. Records are append-only and idempotent; conflicting evidence is preserved as `RECORD_CONFLICT`, never overwritten.

Overwatcher cannot author or alter D0/D1/D2, acceptance counts, candidate content, exemptions, plan meaning, role replacement, or terminal engineering status. A formal work result remains authored by its original role even if Overwatcher notices or reprojects it.

## Communication recovery

Overwatcher observes the existing Supervisor-Checker and Checker-Worker links through transport evidence. It does not intercept normal messages.

For a suspected communication break it must:

1. Inspect the frozen sender, receiver, endpoint version, immutable envelope hash, `message_id`, accepted-delivery evidence, native-start evidence, current Run scope, and legal pause/block state.
2. Stop when matching evidence proves the original receiver already started or completed the work.
3. If the same immutable message was not activated, resend it only to the same registered receiver and endpoint with the same `message_id`.
4. Record the attempt and stop after the bounded recovery policy; never create a replacement role, guess an ID, change content, or create a second delivery identity.
5. Escalate to Supervisor when the receiver is missing, evidence conflicts, retries fail, or any target/content/route/plan change is required.

Neither Overwatcher nor Supervisor uses positive-duration `wait_threads` to stay online. Transport notification is primary and one low-frequency, Run-scoped wake may be used only as a fallback for the optional Overwatcher. It is not evidence of engineering progress.

## TOKEN and BI

SLK TOKEN remains event-driven. Existing role-authored facts plus accepted native delivery advance it through the existing state rules. Overwatcher is neither the sole updater nor a new TOKEN holder; otherwise an unbound Run would be unable to proceed.

No role receives a direct BI edit API. BI is recalculated from work facts, transport evidence, lifecycle facts, and operational observations. Overwatcher may request a re-projection and may cause mechanically derived operational labels such as `ACTIVITY_UNPROVEN`, `DELIVERY_RETRYING`, or `RECORD_CONFLICT` to appear, but it cannot set accepted counts or engineering completion.

An old `WORK_STARTED`, old TOKEN, visible task, heartbeat, or free-text claim is not current activity evidence. `ACTIVE` requires fresh native execution evidence, a new formal event, or an effective legal pause/block/external-wait record. Otherwise the projection becomes `ACTIVITY_UNPROVEN` and Overwatcher recovers or escalates.

## Run identity and duplicate cards

BI identity uses authoritative `project_id + run_id + lineage`, never title text. One Run creates one current card; reconnects, retries, rework, and Session recovery stay inside it. A corrective successor uses one explicit predecessor-successor edge and atomically removes its predecessor from the active set. Independent Runs remain independent.

Multiple current cards for one lineage produce `DUPLICATE_ACTIVE_RUN`; an unverifiable old card produces `ORPHANED_IDENTITY`. Neither is auto-merged or deleted. Overwatcher reports the inconsistency and requests re-projection; only an authorized lifecycle fact changes the active set.

## Interpretation Eval

A versioned closed case pack provides at least 36 release-level scenarios across Supervisor, Checker, Worker, and Overwatcher. It covers the observed errors and semantic variants: online waiting, Supervisor over-participation, direct peer communication, delivery versus acceptance, D1 versus D2, stale activity, exact recovery, optional Overwatcher absence, Overwatcher authority limits, duplicate Runs, legitimate pause/block, headless execution, case/whitespace/extra/missing fields, and harmless project/path changes.

Each Run uses only a short structured readiness subset for its bound roles. Answers come from a closed choice set and are validated mechanically; agents do not self-certify understanding in prose. Full variant coverage remains a repository/release gate so runtime Token cost stays bounded.

## Windows and tool boundaries

OCRV, DSH, Codex helpers, detached transport jobs, and similar child processes use one shared Windows no-window policy. A visible shell requires explicit Owner intent. Tests inspect launch flags without opening real windows.

This release reuses `slk-state`, `slk-transport`, SQLite, and LE BI. It adds no Temporal service, daemon, message broker, MCP server, second database, hidden Agent, or general workflow language. RTK, Repo Map, Headroom, and similar Token tools are not core dependencies; any retained guidance is optional, explicit, and falls back to native commands and raw decisive evidence.

## Minimal-change policy

- Replace Router/Patrol wording instead of appending a parallel control system.
- Reuse exact-endpoint, immutable-message, native-start, evidence, state, projection, lineage, and headless mechanisms already present.
- Remove Router-exclusive TOKEN/BI/next-action authority, mandatory binding, and peer-communication prohibition.
- Keep the main Skill concise; detailed contracts live once in state/transport references and machine-checked fixtures.
- Existing 4.1.1 Runs remain valid and need no Overwatcher migration.

## Acceptance

1. Zero or one Overwatcher is legal; a second binding, cross-Run Session reuse, silent replacement, or unproven endpoint fails closed.
2. A Run with no Overwatcher completes through the original Supervisor/Checker/Worker route.
3. Existing direct communication remains valid and Overwatcher cannot become a mandatory relay.
4. Exact-message retry preserves sender, receiver, endpoint, payload hash, scope, and `message_id`; altered or duplicate work is rejected.
5. Overwatcher cannot author engineering facts, modify TOKEN/BI, change accepted counts, choose routes, or create replacement roles.
6. Work/observation conflicts are preserved and projected without overwriting the authoritative work fact.
7. Old activity evidence becomes `ACTIVITY_UNPROVEN`; legal pause, BLOCKED, and waiting-external states do not trigger false recovery.
8. Explicit lineage yields one active successor; unrelated Runs remain visible; duplicate or orphan identities are reported without destructive cleanup.
9. The full Eval and runtime subset reject all known misinterpretations in normal and optimized Python.
10. OCRV and all related child-process launches are headless on Windows.
11. Rust, Python, BI, repository, schema, migration, manifest/hash, version, mirror, sensitive-data, and base-to-HEAD diff gates pass.
